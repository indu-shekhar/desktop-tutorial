Voice-Based Login

Waiting for "hello indu"...

Step 1: Say "hello indu"

Start the process by saying the activation phrase "hello indu".

Step 2: Provide Your Email

After activation, say your email address clearly.

Step 3: Confirm Your Email

Confirm your email by saying "confirm" or re-enter by saying "cancel".

Step 4: Record Your Voice

Say "record" to start a 5-second voice recording.

Step 5: Capture Your Face

Say "capture face" to start the camera and "snapshot" to take a photo.

Step 6: Submit

Finally, say "confirm" to submit the form or "cancel" to start over.
